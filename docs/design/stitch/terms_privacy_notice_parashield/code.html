1. Scope of Parametric Protection

1.1 Policy Inception & Premium Confirmation: Coverage under the ParaShield Parametric Micro-Income Architecture starts strictly at the Unix timestamp when policy terms take full deterministic effect following formal premium confirmation by the sponsoring platform or direct debit clearance. Advance commitments or pending gateway intents do not trigger institutional underwriting liability.

1.2 Disruption Declaration Threshold: A parametric disruption event (including but not limited to severe precipitation exceedance, wet-bulb heat thresholds, or civil movement restrictions) is declared exclusively when cryptographic observations signed by approved sensory nodes meet the designated numerical threshold and pass quorum validation requirements.

info Important Eligibility Limitation

A declared event alone does not make every worker eligible. Payout settlement requires verified platform geofence presence during the active disruption window, a confirmed shift enrollment state, and prior completion of the dynamic anti-clustering validation protocol.

1.3 Maximum Indemnity Limit: Compensation is capped at the predetermined tranche rate (calculated in Indian Rupees ₹) stated in the individualized active smart contract sheet. Incurred non-income losses, equipment degradation, and secondary enterprise penalties are expressly excluded from parametric coverage.

2. Oracle Data & Quorum Verification

2.1 Decentralized Oracle Ingestion: Environmental indices are collected continuously from authorized telemetry relays. Individual sensor readouts are ingested using verifiable random function (VRF) timing intervals to defend against localized sensor spoofing or deliberate environmental dampening.

2.2 Quorum Agreement Mechanism: No parametric payout state may transition to final execution without verifiable quorum consensus. A quorum is mathematically established when a supermajority of designated independent oracle daemons sign identical temporal-spatial observations within a tolerance margin of ±3.5% across atmospheric variables.

2.3 Fallback & Secondary Auditing: In the event of primary telemetric node latency exceeding 180 seconds, the protocol activates secondary satellite synthetic aperture radar (SAR) feeds. All fallback transitions are recorded in the sandbox state machine with publicly audible root proofs.

4. Data Privacy & Zero PII Exposure

4.1 Data-Minimization Commitments: In accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and sandbox protocols, ParaShield collects only the minimum operational telemetry required to evaluate parametric triggers. We strictly prohibit the retention of raw telephonic records, biometric identifiers, or continuous un-geofenced location histories.

4.2 Cryptographic Anonymization: Delivery partner identifiers, platform driver IDs, and banking tokens are cryptographically hashed using salted SHA-256 protocols before leaving the client interface.

Zero Personally Identifiable Information (Zero PII) Guarantee

Regulator and ecosystem partner views operate strictly with Zero PII. Supervisory dashboards, solvency audits, and platform reconciliation portals only display pseudonymized alphanumeric tokens, cryptographic proofs of presence, and aggregate settlement metrics.
